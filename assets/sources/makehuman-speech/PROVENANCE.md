The original ZIP, reference OBJ, asset license and extracted targets are retained here. No source program code from MakeHuman or MPFB is bundled into the runtime. The application uses derived graphical assets only.

`scripts/retarget-speech.py`, executed with Blender's Python, transfers the 15 Meta/Oculus visemes and seven expression units to each Blender body using nearest-surface triangle barycentrics. Derived sparse position and normal deltas live in `src/assets/speech-morphs.json`. It transfers the source deformation rather than making the vertex numbers of unrelated meshes interchangeable. The result is an automatic transfer and requires artistic refinement, especially lip contact, teeth/tongue and extreme poses. The upstream README itself describes these autogenerated targets as somewhat clunky; CC0 availability is not a realism guarantee.

Runtime weights are applied before smooth body skinning. Speech samples are transient preview state and do not change the saved neutral recipe. Imported timed cues can be Oculus-style `{start,end,viseme,weight?}` arrays or Rhubarb `{mouthCues:[{start,end,value}]}` JSON. Matching local audio drives the cue clock; audio-to-phoneme recognition is not included.

## Blink and oral geometry correction

The MakeHuman blink transfer is replaced with locally generated curved eyelid surfaces fitted to the actual eyeballs. The original eye socket and brows remain unchanged during blink. The sealed lip cap is removed, and recessed mouth walls plus the Blender CC0 `Jaw - Realistic` asset provide separate upper/lower teeth and gingiva. Lower teeth follow the speech jaw motion; upper teeth stay fixed to the skull. These additions are generated by `prepare-facial-geometry.py` and `add-fitted-eyelids.py` before the final speech retarget pass. Lip contact at extremes still needs artistic review.
